Connect your computer

Install the SaveIt4U desktop app once. This extension finds it automatically—no commands, IDs or pairing codes.

  1. Run the SaveIt4U installer

    Open the downloaded SaveIt4U Setup EXE and click Install. Everything needed is included.

  2. Keep this extension installed

    The app and extension connect automatically, regardless of which you install first. If the app was just installed, connection recovery may take a few seconds.

  3. Check your connection

    We reconnect automatically. You can also check immediately below.

Diagnostic details

Use “Choose download folder” in the desktop app to change it. Existing files are never overwritten.
